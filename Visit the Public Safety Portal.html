Our commitment to public safety

At Uber, we are deeply committed to public safety and are always working to help keep our communities safe. That's why we developed this secure portal, which is available 24/7 for law enforcement and public health officials to make emergency requests and get rapid responses from us.

Data that can be obtained from Uber

Each Uber ride and delivery generates data that helps us continue to run Uber efficiently and safely. This includes information submitted through the signup process, needed for use of our services, and required for payment.

About our teams

Public Safety Response

Our dedicated Public Safety Response team actively supports law enforcement and public health officials 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. The global team consists of highly trained public safety specialists who respond to emergency and non-emergency requests through this user-friendly portal.

Public Safety Liaison

Our dedicated Public Safety Liaison team is made up of experienced former law enforcement professionals from around the world who closely work with law enforcement to enhance safety within our communities.
